In-Store Black Friday Hours (No Membership Needed)

Walmart+ early access has never applied to physical retail locations. Walmart stores remain closed on Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26, 2026) and reopen on Black Friday, November 27, at 6:00 a.m. local time.

You can still score top Black Friday discounts without paying for a membership if you prepare a few minutes ahead of time:

  3. Watch the 24-hour restock window: For high-demand "hot Deal drops," check back right at 12:01 a.m. ET the following day when remaining stock unlocks for everyone.
  4. Use free store pickup: Non-members need a $35 minimum order for free shipping, but choosing curbside or in-store pickup avoids shipping fees on smaller orders and often taps into local store inventory.

No. Walmart's terms state that free trial members are not eligible for Black Friday early access—only paid Walmart+ subscribers can shop the exclusive drops.
